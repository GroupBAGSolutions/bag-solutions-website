## What they are

Cookies and local storage are small files your browser keeps to remember information between visits.

## What we use

| Name | Type | Purpose | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| `bag-theme` | Local storage, necessary | Remember whether you prefer the light or dark theme | Until you clear it |
| `bag-consent` | Local storage, necessary | Remember whether you accepted or rejected analytics | Until you clear it |
| `_ga`, `_ga_*` | Analytics cookie (Google Analytics) | Measure visits in aggregate | Up to 2 years |
| `_fbp` | Marketing cookie (Meta Pixel) | Measure Meta campaigns | 3 months |

Analytics and marketing cookies **are only set if you accept them** in the cookie notice. If you reject them, the site works the same.

## Changing your choice

Use the **“Cookie preferences”** link in the footer of any page to see the notice again. You can also delete cookies from your browser settings.
